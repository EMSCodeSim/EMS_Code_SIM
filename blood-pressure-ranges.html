Why it may not be normal

Common reasons a blood pressure reading can change

Not every abnormal number means the same thing. Some are technique issues. Some are temporary stress responses. Some point to real disease or shock.

Technique issuesWrong cuff size, cuff over clothing, arm not at heart level, the patient talking, feet dangling, unsupported back, or deflating too fast.
Temporary causesPain, anxiety, exercise, caffeine, nicotine, a full bladder, or recent activity can raise a reading.
Chronic high BP causesFamily history, age, obesity, sleep apnea, kidney disease, high sodium intake, inactivity, or prescribed medication issues.
Low BP causesDehydration, blood loss, sepsis, endocrine problems, allergic reaction, medication effects, or heart problems.

Signs of a problem

Symptoms that may go with high or low blood pressure

High blood pressure is often called “silent,” while low blood pressure more commonly shows itself through perfusion problems.

↑

High blood pressure clues

  • May have no symptoms at all
  • Severe headache, vision changes, confusion
  • Chest pain or shortness of breath
  • Stroke-like symptoms or altered mental status
↓

Low blood pressure clues

  • Dizziness or near-syncope
  • Weakness, pallor, cool clammy skin
  • Rapid pulse, altered mental status
  • Signs of shock, dehydration, or bleeding
