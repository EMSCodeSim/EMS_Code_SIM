Built for EMS instructors and realistic student practice

The goal is simple: replace passive reading with hands-on assessment reps students can do before, during, or after class.

Why this helps classes

  • Gives students more repetitions with vitals and assessment findings before live skill stations.
  • Creates reusable modules for EMT, paramedic, CPR, ACLS, PALS, and scenario-based training.
  • Supports future instructor workflows: class setup, student tracking, checklists, and printable reports.
  • Keeps the homepage focused while letting new EMS projects plug into the same ecosystem.

Common questions

Is EMSCodeSim a replacement for EMS instructors?

No. It is a training and practice tool designed to support instructor-led education, skill labs, and student review.

Who is this site for?

EMT students, paramedic students, EMS instructors, CPR instructors, and anyone practicing patient assessment or EMS decision-making.

Will more EMS projects be added?

Yes. This page is designed to become the main EMSCodeSim hub for future scenario simulators, quiz tools, skill checklists, and instructor resources.
