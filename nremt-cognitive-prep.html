Current EMT examination structure

The updated EMR and EMT certification examinations launched April 7, 2025. The EMT examination remains computer-adaptive with 70–120 items, including 10 pilot/unscored items, and a two-hour time limit. The current EMT examination is organized around five domains from the 2023 BLS Practice Analysis.

A practical four-week review plan

  1. Week 1 — Scene size-up and primary assessment

    Build a repeatable approach to scene safety, general impression, mental status, airway, breathing, circulation, life threats, priority, and transport decisions. Use the Patient Assessment Center and ABC Center.

  2. Week 2 — Secondary assessment

    Practice SAMPLE, OPQRST, focused exams, and complete vital-sign sets. Use the SAMPLE trainer, OPQRST trainer, and full vitals practice.

  3. Week 3 — Treatment, transport, and reassessment

    Run complete patient scenarios and focus on appropriate interventions, patient response, transport priority, communication, and reassessment. Use the scenario launcher.

  4. Week 4 — Operations and mixed review

    Practice documentation, communication, equipment/operational thinking, and mixed questions. Use the PCR Narrative Coach, timed practice exam, and today’s 5-minute practice.
