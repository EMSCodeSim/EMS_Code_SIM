A practical four-week review plan

  1. Week 1 — Assessment foundations

    Review scene size-up, primary assessment, SAMPLE/OPQRST, and vital signs. Use the ABC center and full vitals practice daily.

  2. Week 2 — Medical & airway

    Rotate asthma, stroke, hypoglycemia scenarios and breath-sound recognition. Take the daily EMT quiz and study missed topics with the Medication Learning Center.

  3. Week 3 — Trauma & operations

    Practice trauma scenarios, splinting logic, hemorrhage/shock concepts, consent/refusal, and documentation with the Trauma Learning Center and PCR narrative coach.

  4. Week 4 — Mixed review & psychomotor alignment

    Take a timed practice exam, use today’s 5-minute practice, repeat weak flashcards, and walk through official skill sheets before your practical exam.

NREMT cognitive prep FAQ

Is EMSCodeSim affiliated with NREMT?

No. This is an independent free study site. Always follow your approved program and official National Registry guidance.

Should I only take practice tests?

No. Use a practice exam to find weak domains, then drill those topics with quizzes, learning centers, and scenarios so you build understanding—not just recognition.

How often should I practice?

Short daily drills plus one timed exam each week is a practical cadence for most EMT students in the final review window.
