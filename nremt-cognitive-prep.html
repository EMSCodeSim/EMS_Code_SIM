NREMT EMT cognitive exam prep

Use topic-based daily quizzes, patient scenarios, flashcards, and skill sheets alongside your approved EMT course—not as a replacement for it.

How the cognitive exam is organized

The National Registry EMT cognitive exam covers six major content areas. EMSCodeSim groups free practice tools under each area so you can study weak topics deliberately instead of only taking random quizzes.

NREMT content areaWhat to practice here
Airway, Respiration & VentilationBreath sound center, lung sound simulator, airway quiz questions
Cardiology & ResuscitationCPR training center, pulse trainer, cardiology quiz questions
Traumapatient scenarios, Rule of Nines, trauma quiz questions
MedicalABC learning center, glucose simulator, medical quiz questions
Obstetrics & GynecologyAPGAR simulator, OB/GYN quiz questions
EMS Operationsprotocol drill, skill sheets, operations quiz questions

A practical four-week review plan

  1. Week 1 — Assessment foundations

    Review scene size-up, primary assessment, SAMPLE/OPQRST, and vital signs. Use the ABC center and full vitals practice daily.

  2. Week 2 — Medical & airway

    Rotate asthma, stroke, hypoglycemia scenarios and breath-sound recognition. Take the daily EMT quiz and study missed topics.

  3. Week 3 — Trauma & operations

    Practice trauma scenarios, splinting logic, hemorrhage/shock concepts, consent/refusal, and documentation with the PCR narrative coach.

  4. Week 4 — Mixed review & psychomotor alignment

    Use today’s 5-minute practice, repeat weak flashcards, and walk through official skill sheets before your practical exam.

Independent study resource

EMSCodeSim is not affiliated with the National Registry. Use your program, textbooks, and instructor feedback as the primary source of truth for exam readiness.
