# Rapid Primary Assessment Update

- Airway, breathing, and circulation are classified directly on the patient screen.
- The scenario finding is no longer shown before the learner makes a choice.
- Normal findings save immediately and keep the learner on the patient screen.
- Abnormal findings are recorded and detailed assessment/treatment links remain available.
- Learner choices include expected-normality and accuracy metadata for debrief grading.
- Learning Mode provides brief confirmation without revealing the answer first.
- Assessment Mode records the decision for end-of-scenario review.
