EMT flashcards by category—or completely random

Build a focused deck or mix everything together. Review foundational terms before EMT school and keep them fresh during class.

What is included

This starter library covers anatomy and physiology, medical terminology, common EMS jargon, equipment, street-drug awareness, EMS medications, patient assessment, vital signs, operations, and safety.

Designed to support—not replace—EMT education. Drug recognition never confirms what a substance contains. Medication scope, radio codes, equipment use, and protocols vary by state and agency.

Future supporting tools and app packs

The flashcard system is built so new decks can be added later without rebuilding the page.

Photo equipment identification

Future app support can ask learners to identify airway, trauma, oxygen, and monitoring equipment from real photos.

Audio and radio terminology

Future listening exercises can reinforce dispatch phrases, radio reports, breath sounds, and pronunciation.

Spaced-repetition accounts

A future app or login can sync difficult cards, scheduled reviews, and custom instructor decks across devices.

Authoritative references used for deck boundaries

These cards stay at a foundation and awareness level. They intentionally avoid medication dosing and local treatment authorization.
