What larger departments may request

The exact sequence and required documents vary. Always follow the current posting and the department’s official instructions.

Initial application

Basic qualifications

  • Legal and contact information
  • Education and work experience
  • Driver-license and work-authorization eligibility
  • Fire, EMS, instructor, apparatus, or specialty credentials
  • Position, location, schedule, and relocation preferences
Testing

Hiring-process readiness

  • Written or aptitude testing
  • CPAT or another physical-ability test
  • Structured interview or oral board
  • Skills verification where applicable
  • Current test dates, scores, and expiration dates
Background

Complete history

  • Past addresses and employers
  • Supervisors and reasons for leaving
  • Driving, legal, military, and prior-application history
  • Personal and professional references
  • Truthful disclosure of requested background information
Documentation

Records to locate early

  • Résumé and adaptable cover letter
  • Diploma, GED, degrees, and transcripts
  • Licenses and certificates
  • CPAT documentation
  • DD214 when applicable
Conditional stages

Later hiring steps

  • Document and eligibility verification
  • Fingerprints and background investigation
  • Medical, psychological, or drug screening
  • Final review and conditional offer requirements
Competitive preparation

Evidence beyond minimums

  • Leadership and teamwork examples
  • Teaching, mentoring, projects, and committees
  • Documented training and professional growth
  • Examples of judgment, accountability, and improvement

A practical 90-day advancement plan

Days 1–30

Build the foundation

Choose a target, study several real postings, update job history, verify credential dates, contact references, and identify the two largest readiness gaps.

Days 31–60

Close important gaps

Schedule needed testing or training, collect documents, improve the résumé, and record strong examples of leadership, judgment, teamwork, and learning.

Days 61–90

Practice the process

Complete a mock application, rehearse oral-board answers, verify every date, monitor openings, and maintain a calendar for deadlines and testing.

Prepare stronger interview examples

Use Situation–Task–Action–Result, but keep the setup brief. Explain what you personally did, the result, and what you learned.

Leadership

A time you took responsibility, set direction, supported a team, or improved an outcome without relying on rank.

Accountability

A real mistake or weakness, how you addressed it, and the specific change that followed.

Conflict and communication

A disagreement handled professionally while protecting safety, service, policy, and working relationships.

Protect sensitive information

Do not save Social Security numbers, banking data, medical records, passwords, or identity-document numbers in ordinary notes or career logs. Enter requested sensitive details only through the department’s verified, secure hiring system.
