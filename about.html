Experience behind the resource

About EMSCodeSim

Free EMS career guidance and readiness tools built by an experienced paramedic, firefighter, and instructor.

Why EMSCodeSim exists

EMSCodeSim was created to give future and current EMS professionals one free resource they can use from first interest in the career through school, field practice, recertification, career growth, wellness, and retirement planning.

The site is intentionally different from an approved EMT program or a commercial certification-preparation course. It helps visitors understand the path, enter training better prepared, and practice individual concepts without claiming to replace instructors, clinical education, local protocols, or certification requirements.

About David Gallaher

David Gallaher has more than two decades of paramedic experience and more than 17 years in the fire service. His teaching work includes EMT education, CPR, ACLS, PALS, skills testing, and practical examination support.

That background shapes the site’s emphasis on simple explanations, realistic expectations, repeated practice, patient safety, and clear reminders to confirm current state, program, employer, and medical-direction requirements.

Editorial standards

Stay in scope

Readiness pages prepare students for approved education. Simulator pages reinforce concepts. Neither is presented as certification or authorization to practice.

Prefer official sources

Certification, licensing, financial aid, workforce, and state-requirement guidance should link to the responsible government, accrediting, or certifying organization whenever possible.

Show review dates

Major guides include a review date so visitors know when requirements, fees, or external links should be verified again.

Correct openly

Substantive errors are corrected when identified. Visitors are encouraged to report broken links, outdated requirements, or unclear clinical wording.

Medical and education disclaimer

EMSCodeSim is an independent educational resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians, the American Heart Association, a state EMS authority, or a specific education program unless a page explicitly states otherwise.

Protocols, scopes of practice, licensing, certification, course requirements, medications, documentation standards, and patient-care expectations vary. Confirm current information with your program, state EMS office, employer, medical director, and applicable official sources.

Submit a correction or suggestion

Email EMSCodeSim@gmail.com with the page address, the wording or link that needs attention, and an official source when available.
