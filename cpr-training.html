The core response

Adult sudden collapse: four priorities

This simple sequence is designed for recognition and recall. A formal class teaches physical technique, special situations, team roles, and skills testing.

1

Recognize

Check safety. If the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally or is only gasping, assume cardiac arrest.

2

Call

Activate 911 and send someone for an AED. Put the phone on speaker when possible and follow dispatcher directions.

3

Compress

Push hard and fast in the center of the chest. Aim for 100–120 per minute and allow full recoil.

4

Defibrillate

Turn on the AED, attach pads, follow prompts, clear for analysis or shock, and immediately resume CPR.
