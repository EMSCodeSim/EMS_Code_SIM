Colorado enrollment checklist

  1. Confirm that the exact EMT course meets current Colorado education requirements.
  2. Ask what is required for labs and clinical placement, including BLS, background checks, drug screening, immunizations, and insurance.
  3. Ask which costs fall outside advertised tuition.
  4. Confirm what graduates must complete for National Registry and Colorado certification.
