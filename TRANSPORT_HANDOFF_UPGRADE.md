# Transport and Handoff Upgrade

## Added
- Transport tab on the patient-picture home screen.
- Scenario-specific working-impression choices.
- Transport priority and destination selection.
- Rationale field for clinical reasoning.
- Handoff-readiness checklist based on actual scenario phases.
- Handoff generator using obtained findings, vitals, treatments, and reassessments.
- Editable handoff report saved to the patient record.
- Direct link to the full PCR/verbal handoff tool.
- Transport decisions included in the care log and final debrief data.

## Scenario destination logic
- Respiratory distress: appropriate emergency department.
- Stroke: stroke-capable center.
- Hypoglycemia: appropriate emergency department with escalation when high risk.
- Trauma: trauma center.
- Pediatric respiratory illness: pediatric-capable emergency department.
