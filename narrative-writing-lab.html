Beginner writing checklist
  1. Start with dispatch, scene, and how the patient was found.
  2. Document the chief complaint and relevant history.
  3. Record objective assessment findings and initial vitals.
  4. Describe care in chronological order.
  5. Include response, repeat findings, disposition, and transfer.
