Why this beats a long question bank

Retention comes from a daily habit people finish and talk about — not from dumping 50 questions on one page.

  • Micro-session length (about two minutes) so it’s easy to finish on a break.
  • Instant feedback so every miss becomes a teaching moment.
  • Completion streaks that create a reason to return tomorrow.
  • Shareable results that invite classmates without giving away answers.
  • Free, no login, phone-first — same questions all day so you can resume later.

EMSCodeSim Daily Quiz FAQ

Common questions about the free EMT and paramedic practice tools.

Is EMSCodeSim Daily Quiz free?

Yes. The daily EMT quiz, paramedic quiz, EMS fact, MedWord, and medication review tools are available without a login.

How do streaks work?

Your streak increases when you finish today’s EMT or Paramedic quiz. Visiting the hub alone does not count. Miss a day and the streak resets to 1 on your next completion.

Can I share my score?

Yes. After you finish, use Share to post a spoiler-safe emoji grid with your score and streak — or copy it to the clipboard.

Is this an official NREMT site?

No. EMSCodeSim Daily Quiz is an independent EMS study resource and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians.

Who is this site for?

It is designed for EMT students, paramedic students, EMS instructors, and providers who want short, repeatable EMS review.
