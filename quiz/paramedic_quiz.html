Sample Paramedic practice questions

These examples show the style of questions used on this page and help search engines understand the educational content.

1. A 36-year-old female presents with right-sided weakness, slurred speech, and facial droop that started 30 minutes ago. Her blood glucose is 98 mg/dL. What is the most appropriate next action?

Answer: Transport to a stroke center immediately

2. A 50-year-old male has circumferential burns to his chest and is struggling to breathe. Breath sounds are decreased bilaterally. What is your most immediate concern?

Answer: Impaired chest wall expansion

3. A combative patient is threatening bystanders and screaming unintelligibly. Vitals are normal. What is the safest initial management?

Answer: Ensure scene safety and wait for law enforcement
