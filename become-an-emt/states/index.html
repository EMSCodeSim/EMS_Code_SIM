How to become an EMT — state by state

High-search guides for requirements, costs, approved programs, NREMT vs. state licensing, and reciprocity. Start with Texas or pick another top state, then jump into free prep, simulators, and the career planner.

National overview first

NREMT certification is not a state license

Almost every state uses National Registry testing in some form—but permission to practice comes from the state EMS office (or, in California, a Local EMS Agency). Use the national roadmap, then open your state guide before you enroll.
