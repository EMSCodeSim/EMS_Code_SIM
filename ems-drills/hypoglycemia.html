Hypoglycemia Management

Recognize hypoglycemia, decide on oral glucose vs advanced options conceptually, and reassess mentation.

Certification: EMT, AEMT, Paramedic · Type: Individual · Completion: Self-completed

Start Drill

What you will practice

  • Link mentation to glucose testing
  • Protect airway during treatment decisions
  • Reassess after glucose

Educational boundary

General educational material for training. Confirm exact indications, doses, destinations, and documentation requirements with your local protocol and medical direction. Exact glucose cutoffs and ALS meds are local/scope-specific.
