Medical Patient Assessment

Work a medical primary survey, focused history, vitals interpretation, and transport priority decision.

Certification: EMT, AEMT, Paramedic · Type: Individual · Completion: Self-completed

Start Drill

What you will practice

  • Perform a logical primary survey order
  • Collect focused SAMPLE and OPQRST information
  • Recognize findings that change priority
  • Document the reassessment trigger

Educational boundary

General educational material for training. Confirm exact indications, doses, destinations, and documentation requirements with your local protocol and medical direction.
