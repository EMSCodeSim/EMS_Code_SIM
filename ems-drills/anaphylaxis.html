Anaphylaxis Recognition & Response

Recognize anaphylaxis, prioritize epinephrine conceptually, and support airway/breathing.

Certification: EMT, AEMT, Paramedic · Type: Individual · Completion: Self-completed

Start Drill

What you will practice

  • Recognize multi-system involvement
  • Prioritize epinephrine when authorized
  • Support ABCs and reassess

Educational boundary

General educational material for training. Confirm exact indications, doses, destinations, and documentation requirements with your local protocol and medical direction. Epinephrine dose/device authorization is local and scope-specific.
