Respiratory Distress Assessment

Differentiate distress from failure, choose oxygen/ventilation support, and reassess work of breathing.

Certification: EMT, AEMT, Paramedic · Type: Individual · Completion: Self-completed

Start Drill

What you will practice

  • Recognize distress versus failure cues
  • Select an appropriate oxygen/ventilation approach
  • Reassess after support

Educational boundary

General educational material for training. Confirm exact indications, doses, destinations, and documentation requirements with your local protocol and medical direction.
