# Assessment Tab Clinical Order Update — v1.4.1

## What changed

- Scene size-up no longer opens automatically when a visual patient scenario begins.
- The Assessment tab now follows a fixed clinical sequence:
  1. Scene size-up and first impression
  2. Airway
  3. Breathing
  4. Circulation and perfusion
  5. Focused assessment and history
- Scene size-up remains near the top so learners repeatedly see the NREMT-style thinking order without being forced into the guide.
- Each primary-assessment card separates:
  - what is visible or reasonably known now;
  - why that conclusion is supported;
  - what remains unknown and requires a vital-sign or assessment tool.
- Primary cards include direct links to the appropriate connected simulators.

## Respiratory distress example

- Speaking in short sentences supports a patent airway at that moment.
- Upright posture, anxiety, and short sentences show that breathing is present and labored.
- Respiratory rate, depth, chest rise, breath sounds, and SpO2 remain unknown until assessed.
- Being awake and speaking supports the inference that a perfusing pulse is present.
- Pulse rate/quality, blood pressure, skin signs, capillary refill, and hidden bleeding remain unknown until assessed.
