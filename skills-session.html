      Step 2 · five-minute warm-up

      Take, say, and chart one full vital-sign set

      For each item: obtain the value or finding, say it clearly, identify whether it changes priority, and document it without inventing information.

      HRRate, rhythm, quality
      RRRate, rhythm, quality, effort
      BPSystolic/diastolic, arm, position
      SpO₂Reading and oxygen device
      SkinColor, temperature, moisture
      PupilsSize, equality, reactivity
      BGLValue and unit
      PainScore, location, change

      Say it: “Pulse 104, regular and strong. Respirations 22, regular with increased effort. Blood pressure 138 over 84…”

      Step 3 · write the call

      Document after the skill, then revise once

      Use only facts obtained during the fictional case. Get completeness feedback, correct missing or unsupported details, and submit one improved version.

      CHART mini-template

      C: Dispatched for ___; patient reports ___.

      H: Onset/events ___; SAMPLE ___.

      A: General impression ___; ABCs ___; vitals/findings ___.

      R: Care provided ___; patient response ___.

      T: Transport priority/destination ___; transfer of care ___.

      Chronological mini-template

      “Dispatched to ___ for ___. Found patient ___. Primary assessment showed ___. History included ___. Initial vitals were ___. EMS provided ___. On reassessment ___. Patient was transported ___ and care transferred to ___.”
