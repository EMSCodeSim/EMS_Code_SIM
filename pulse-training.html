Four pulse qualities

What are you actually assessing?

#

Rate

How many beats occur in one minute. The expected rate changes with age, exercise, illness, medications, and conditioning.

≈

Rhythm

Are the beats evenly spaced, mostly regular, or irregular? An irregular pulse should be counted for a full minute.

↕

Strength

Does the pulse feel absent, weak/thready, normal, strong, or bounding? Strength gives clues about perfusion and stroke volume.

=

Equality

Do matching pulse sites feel the same on both sides? Unequal peripheral pulses can be clinically important.
