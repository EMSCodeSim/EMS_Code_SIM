Blood Pressure

Interpret systolic and diastolic pressure with technique, perfusion, and patient context.

App adult practice: about 90–119 / 60–79

Pulse

Assess rate, rhythm, quality, and location—not rate alone.

Typical adult: about 60–100 BPM

Respirations

Count quietly and describe rate, rhythm, depth, effort, speech, and chest movement.

Typical adult: about 12–20/min

Skin Signs

Document color, temperature, and moisture together and compare with baseline.

Typical description: warm, pink, dry
Learn and practice skin signs →

Pupils

Document size, equality, shape, reactivity, and patient-left versus patient-right.

Reference: PERRL
Learn and practice pupils →

SpO₂

Verify signal quality and compare the reading with breathing, perfusion, and mental status.

Typical healthy adult: about 95–100%

Blood Glucose

Use BGL during altered mental status and diabetic presentations, then decide what the number explains.

App bands: <70 low; 70–180 reference; 181–350 high; >350 concerning
Learn and practice BGL →

AVPU

Rapidly classify level of consciousness as Alert, Verbal, Pain, or Unresponsive.

Typical awake adult: Alert

A&O×4
