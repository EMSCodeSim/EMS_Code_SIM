Skin signs
  • Pallor: low perfusion/shock, anemia.
  • Cyanosis: hypoxemia.
  • Flushed: fever, vasodilation, CO₂ retention.
  • Jaundice: bilirubin elevation.
  • Mottled: poor perfusion/cold stress.
  • Rash: infection, allergy, drug reaction (pattern dependent).
CRT

Press & hold the skin to blanch, then release. The timer stops at ~90% recovery. Adjust “Target refill” and “Abnormal ≥” as needed.

Turgor

Use Pinch Skin to raise a ridge; release to time recoil. Longer recoil suggests dehydration/aging skin changes.
