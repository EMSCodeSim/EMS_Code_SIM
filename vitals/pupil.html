About this Pupil Simulator
  • Dilation Ratio = pupil diameter ÷ iris diameter (shown per eye).
  • Shapes: Round, Vertical Slit, Horizontal Oval, Irregular.
  • Light Reflex: constrict → redilate; can be set to sluggish or non-reactive by the case.
  • Quiz Mode: Randomized cases (pinpoint, dilated, anisocoria, sluggish, non-reactive/blown, irregular).
  • Pupil Size Chart: quick visual for 1–8 mm reference.

Training visual only—use clinically with full neuro exam, context, and protocols.
