About this Simulator

This trainer randomizes realistic, protocol-aligned pupil findings per eye (no manual controls). Test light response and select the correct finding for each eye.

  • Findings include: Normal, Pinpoint, Dilated, Sluggish, Non-reactive/Blown, Unequal, Irregular.
  • Cases map to patterns seen with opioid OD, stimulant OD, head injury, stroke, hypoxia, 3rd nerve palsy, post-surgical pupils, etc.
  • Results include likely reasons/associations for your chosen findings.

Educational use only — correlate with full neuro exam, lighting conditions, medications, and your local protocols.
