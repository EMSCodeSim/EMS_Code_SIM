About this Pupil Simulator
  • Dilation Ratio = pupil diameter ÷ iris diameter (shown for each eye).
  • Shapes: Round, Vertical Slit (very constricted), Horizontal Oval (trauma/pressure variant), Irregular (post-surgical/trauma).
  • Light Reflex animates constriction then redilation. Use per eye or for both.
  • Anisocoria flag trips when left/right ratios differ by ≥10 percentage points.

Use clinically with context (lighting, medications, trauma, neuro exam). This is a training visual, not a diagnostic device.
