How this works

Press Start, palpate/count the simulated pulse for 30 seconds, then enter the rate in beats per minute. The timer does not stop at 30 seconds; it keeps running until you record the pulse.

For a regular pulse, counting 30 seconds and multiplying the beat count by two is a common EMS technique. If the rhythm is irregular, count for a full minute and assess the rhythm carefully.
