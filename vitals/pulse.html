How this works

Press Start to generate a random heart rhythm (30–200 BPM). Listen to the beeps and watch the heart animation, then pick your best guess with the slider and tap Check Answer.

  • <60 BPM: bradycardic range
  • 60–100 BPM: normal resting range
  • >100 BPM: tachycardic range

Tap New Rhythm for another try. Audio unlocks on your first tap (iOS friendly).
