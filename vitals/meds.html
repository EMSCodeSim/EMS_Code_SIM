The 5 Rights (EMS)
  1. Right Patient — Correct identity and the medication is indicated for this patient; if assisting, the bottle label name matches the patient.
  2. Right Medication — Correct drug and correct formulation/concentration/device.
  3. Right Dose — Correct amount for age/weight and correct total/count when applicable (e.g., Nitro max, Albuterol puffs).
  4. Right Route — Correct route and delivery device for the medication.
  5. Right Time — Appropriate to give now; not expired; proper interval since last dose; not over maximum total doses.

Always also check Contraindications and document the medication, dose, route, time, and patient response.
