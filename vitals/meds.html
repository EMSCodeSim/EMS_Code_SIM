About this Trainer

This tool drills the 5 Rights (Right Patient, Right Medication, Right Dose, Right Route, Right Time).

  • Scenarios may include a bottle label with a name that does not match the patient. That’s a safety check.
  • It also checks indications/contraindications (e.g., blood pressure, ED meds, ability to swallow, active bleeding, etc.).
  • Follow-up questions appear where appropriate (e.g., Nitro max doses and interval).
