How to use the Breath Sound Simulator
  • Rotate Body: Switch among FRONT → RIGHT → BACK → LEFT views.
  • Tap Regions: Press a labeled spot (ULL/URL/LLL/LRL, Neck, Midaxillary) to play sounds.
  • Choose Sounds: Set Left and Right to Normal or Wheeze, then tap a side.
  • Tip (iPhone): First tap unlocks audio; use the on-screen controls as needed.
