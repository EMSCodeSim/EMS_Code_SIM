How to use

Practice upper and lower lung fields on an anatomical anterior and posterior chest.

  • Choose a sound for the left and right lungs.
  • Tap RUL, LUL, RLL, or LLL on the chest.
  • Switch Anterior / Posterior to compare matching fields.
  • Compare side-to-side before you decide what you hear.
