How to use this simulator

Practice systematic lung auscultation the way you would on a patient.

  • Rotate through front, right, back, and left views.
  • Assign different sounds to left and right lung fields.
  • Tap each site and listen through a full respiratory cycle.
  • Stridor is only available at the neck.
  • Compare side-to-side before you decide what you hear.
