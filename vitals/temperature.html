Practice point: Record the actual value, units, time, route/device, and patient context. Temperature varies with route, device, age, environment, and clinical condition.

Training categories use clear examples rather than diagnosing a patient. For general reference, CDC commonly defines fever as 100.4°F (38°C) or greater, while MedlinePlus describes body temperature below 95°F (35°C) as hypothermia. Follow your course and local clinical guidance.
