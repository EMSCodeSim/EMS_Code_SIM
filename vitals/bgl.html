What the Reading Means

Hypoglycemia

  • < 70 mg/dL: treat as hypoglycemia on the exam; assess and manage per protocols. (teaching threshold)
  • ≤ 60 mg/dL: many EMS protocols use this as a treatment trigger (oral glucose if able; D10/D50/Glucagon per scope).
  • < 45 mg/dL: high risk for severe neuroglycopenia; prompt dextrose indicated.

Normal (field reading)

  • Common teaching window: ~80–140 mg/dL (non-fasting field checks vary).

Hyperglycemia

  • > 180 mg/dL: hyperglycemia flag. Assess for infection/illness, dehydration, DKA/HHS risk with very high readings.

These are NREMT-prep friendly ranges; always follow your local protocol specifics during patient care.
