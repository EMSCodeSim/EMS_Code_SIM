# EMT Prep Vital Signs Skill Lab Update

## Purpose
The Vital Signs Foundations module was redesigned so the "how to" portions are the primary learning experience rather than a reading lesson followed by a quiz.

## Major changes
- Expanded the module to an estimated 150–190 minutes.
- Added eight guided skill labs: blood pressure, pulse, respirations, pulse oximetry, skin signs, blood glucose, pupils/mental status, and temperature.
- Every lab includes clickable technique steps, a common-error challenge, a direct simulator assignment, an objective documentation entry, and saved completion progress.
- Added a patient-case generator and complete vital-sign reporting capstone.
- Kept the final quiz as a short confirmation rather than the main activity.
- Updated the main EMT Prep dashboard total to 11–14 hours.
