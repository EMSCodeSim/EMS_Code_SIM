Why it may not be normal

Common reasons the pulse changes

Normal body responseExercise, standing up, stress, fear, excitement, pain, heat, and fever can increase the pulse.
Volume or perfusion problemDehydration, bleeding, shock, sepsis, or allergic reaction may cause a fast, weak pulse.
Medication or substance effectBeta blockers and some calcium channel blockers may slow the pulse; stimulants, nicotine, caffeine, and some drugs may speed it up.
Electrical or heart problemArrhythmias, conduction disease, heart attack, electrolyte imbalance, or other cardiac conditions can change rate and rhythm.
