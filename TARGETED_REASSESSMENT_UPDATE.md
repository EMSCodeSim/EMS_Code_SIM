# EMSCodeSim Targeted Reassessment Update

## New clinical flow

- Treatments are linked to the assessment areas they affect.
- The patient-home assessment card changes to **Reassessment due** after treatment.
- Selecting **Reassess** returns to the affected assessment with a new post-treatment finding.
- The learner compares the new finding with baseline as **Improved**, **Unchanged**, or **Worsened**.
- The reassessment is saved to the chronological patient-care log.
- The patient-home card then displays the response state.
- Immediate patient responses remain visible in the top information-update window but do not count as the required formal reassessment.

## Airway example

1. Abnormal airway finding identified.
2. Head-tilt/chin-lift selected.
3. Treatment is logged.
4. Patient response appears in the information window.
5. Airway and breathing are marked **Reassessment due**.
6. The learner repeats the affected assessment and records whether the patient improved, remained unchanged, or worsened.
