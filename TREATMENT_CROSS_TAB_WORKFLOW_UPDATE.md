# Treatment and Cross-Tab Workflow Update

- Treatment options are organized into Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Medications, Trauma, Transport Actions, and Other Care.
- The menu no longer identifies indicated, contraindicated, or recommended options before the learner decides.
- Treatment cards no longer display clinical checks or reassessment answers before treatment.
- All available treatment choices are shown by category rather than sorted according to the patient diagnosis.
- Abnormal assessment findings open a neutral next-action choice: Treat finding, Obtain vitals, or Return to patient.
- Treat finding opens the Treatment tab and expands the category related to the documented abnormality.
- Obtain vitals opens the Vitals tab without losing the assessment finding.
