PCR Narrative Coach

Answer relevant questions, skip what does not apply, and build an organized EMS narrative without allowing the system to invent normal findings or undocumented facts.

Do not enter patient identifiers. Do not enter names, dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, incident numbers, report numbers, exact locations, or identifying narratives. This tool is not an approved ePCR, legal medical record, agency QA system, or substitute for local documentation policy.

1. Select a call template

You can change templates without losing common answers.

2. Document what happened

Use a status choice when a question is not applicable, unknown, or not assessed. A blank answer is never converted into a denial or normal finding.

3. Review the report

Verify every sentence before using it.

Completeness and quality review

Documentation principle: describe what the patient reported, what another historian reported, what EMS observed, what EMS assessed, what EMS did, and how the patient responded. Follow your agency’s approved abbreviations, local protocol, state requirements, medical direction, and ePCR rules.
