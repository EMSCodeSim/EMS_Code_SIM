# Scenario-Aware Treatment Update

- Treatment choices are evaluated against the findings already obtained.
- Indicated treatments appear first.
- Options that need more assessment, are not indicated, or are contraindicated remain available for clinical decision practice and are clearly labeled.
- Every treatment records elapsed scenario time, indication status, outcome classification, patient response, and targeted reassessment areas.
- Appropriate treatment marks the affected assessments/vitals as due for reassessment.
- Premature, unnecessary, and contraindicated choices are logged without falsely improving the patient.
