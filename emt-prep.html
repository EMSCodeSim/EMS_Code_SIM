Free pre-course foundation

Get ready for EMT school before day one

Build the language, anatomy, vital-sign, assessment, communication, and study foundations that make a fast-paced EMT course easier to enter and easier to manage.

This prepares you for EMT school—it does not replace it

EMSCodeSim’s EMT Prep Program is a free readiness resource for future students. It does not provide certification, course credit, protocol authorization, clinical training, or a substitute for a state-approved EMT education program. Your EMT instructors should remain the source of truth for course requirements, skills, treatment standards, and local protocols.

What this program covers

You will build

  • Basic medical vocabulary and directional language
  • A practical overview of major body systems
  • Understanding of what common vital signs represent
  • Familiarity with the patient-assessment sequence
  • Study, communication, and time-management habits
  • A realistic picture of EMT-school expectations

You will not be asked to

  • Memorize a complete EMT textbook before class
  • Learn local treatment protocols on your own
  • Practice invasive or medication skills without instruction
  • Replace hands-on lab time with an online page
  • Use this program as proof of certification or competency
