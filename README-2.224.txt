What changed
1. Desktop History now keeps one history group selected until the learner selects a different group.
2. The fixed right-side Patient Question panel displays every available question in the selected group as direct-click buttons. There is no Choose Question dropdown or separate Ask button on desktop.
3. Asked questions remain visible with a check mark and may be asked again.
4. History groups are now:
   - Patient Information
   - Current Problem
   - SAMPLE
   - OPQRST
   - Mechanism / Trauma
   - Risk / Baseline Function
5. Added horse-scenario interview questions for fictional patient demographics and clinically relevant trauma history, including name, DOB, age, home/address context, emergency contact, other injuries, numbness/tingling, nausea/dizziness, blood thinners, prior hip/leg injury, baseline mobility, whether the patient was stepped on, and whether she was moved before ambulance arrival.
6. The Patient Update window continues to display the patient's answer immediately after a question is clicked, with patient-dialogue TTS behavior preserved.
7. Documented name/DOB/age can now populate the hospital handoff notes. If the learner does not ask for those items, the handoff does not reveal hidden demographic answers.

Scenario demographic values are fictional training data.
