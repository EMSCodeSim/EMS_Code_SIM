# Treatment Decision and Transport Update

- Removes the standalone Transport bottom tab.
- Adds transport as a Treatment category with working impression, emergent/non-emergent priority, receiving destination, specialty activation, and rationale.
- Requires treatment-specific documentation before an intervention can be recorded.
- Medication treatments require medication, dose, and route where applicable.
- Oxygen and ventilation treatments require device and flow/rate details.
- Incorrect treatments and transport choices are recorded for final debrief without revealing the answer before commitment.
