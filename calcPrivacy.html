Overview

EMSCodeSim (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices when using our application.

EMSCodeSim is designed as a training and simulation tool for EMS and fire personnel. We aim to collect the minimum amount of data necessary to operate and improve the app.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily enter, such as:

  • Name (for class or report tracking)
  • Email address (for exports or communication)
  • Training data (scenarios, reports, evaluations)

2. Automatically Collected Information

We may collect limited technical data, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

3. Local Storage

Most data (presets, department setup, scenarios, reports) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted unless you choose to export or share it.

How We Use Information

  • Provide and operate the app
  • Improve performance and features
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Support training tools

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share limited data only:

  • With service providers (analytics or crash reporting)

  • To protect users or prevent misuse

Third-Party Services

EMSCodeSim may use third-party services such as analytics or crash reporting tools. These services may collect anonymized data according to their own policies.

Advertising

If ads are used:

  • These providers may use cookies or similar technologies

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.

Your Choices

  • You can choose not to enter personal information
  • You can adjust device privacy settings where available
