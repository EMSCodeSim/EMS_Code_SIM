# Dynamic Patient Condition Engine

- Evaluates patient progression only at safe UI checkpoints.
- Uses elapsed scenario time, patient-specific stages, and completed effective treatments.
- Adds condition-change alerts to the information window and care log.
- Marks only affected assessment/vital targets as due for reassessment.
- Supports blocked deterioration when time-sensitive care is completed.
- Adds visual condition-state cues without continuously mutating the patient record.
