# Repeatable Clinical Workflow Update

- Adds chief-complaint sorting to Assessment without revealing the diagnosis.
- Makes completed assessments and vital signs repeatable through Reassess actions.
- Allows repeated partner vital assignments.
- Allows treatments and medication doses to be performed more than once; every administration is separately logged.
- Adds a broad EMT-level treatment library. Local protocols and medical direction remain controlling.
- Keeps transport inside Treatment and removes any separate transport navigation.
- Adds End Scenario and Reset Scenario controls.
