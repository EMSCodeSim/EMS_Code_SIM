Medical Terminology Foundations

Foundation only

This module prepares you to enter an approved EMT course. It does not provide certification, course credit, local protocols, or authorization to perform EMS skills.

Learn the building blocks of medical terminology

Focus on decoding unfamiliar words instead of memorizing long lists without context.

PrefixAppears at the beginning and often describes amount, speed, location, or condition.
RootProvides the central meaning, often a body part or system.
SuffixAppears at the end and often describes a condition, process, or procedure.
