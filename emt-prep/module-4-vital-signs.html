Preview vital signs with guided practice

Learn what each measurement represents, then use the simulator to become comfortable with the process.

Start herePulse TrainerCount beats, calculate a rate, and connect rate with rhythm and strength. Vital signsBlood Pressure SimulatorPreview cuff control, Korotkoff sounds, and systolic versus diastolic pressure. OxygenationPulse Oximeter SimulatorRecognize the displayed SpO₂ and pulse values while remembering that devices have limits. MetabolicBlood Glucose SimulatorPreview meter workflow and what a blood glucose result represents. ObservationSkin Signs SimulatorPractice describing color, temperature, moisture, and capillary refill. Optional previewBreath Sound SimulatorBecome familiar with normal and abnormal sound names without trying to master interpretation yet.

Use the ranges and technique taught by your EMT program. A number must always be interpreted with patient appearance, history, trends, and the limits of the device or technique.
