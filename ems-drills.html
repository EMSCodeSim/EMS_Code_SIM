Training experience, not another LMS

EMSCodeSim provides the interactive drill. Responder Roadmap can assign the drill, track completion, and keep department accountability. Your agency RMS remains the long-term official record when you use that workflow.

Drill library

Choose a drill and start

Filter by category, certification level, crew type, or completion style. Every card shows time, difficulty, and whether the drill is self-completed or evaluator-verified.

Expandable categories

Built for growth

Categories are data-driven so new drills can be added without rebuilding the site. Initial coverage spans assessment through documentation.

General education vs local protocol

EMS Drills teach repeatable assessment and decision habits. They do not silently invent your local medication doses, destination plans, or documentation rules. When a choice depends on agency policy, the drill says so and points you back to medical direction.

Responder Roadmap assignments

Departments using Responder Roadmap can assign an EMSCodeSim drill. Members tap Start Drill, complete the activity here, and a signed completion event returns to Roadmap. Standalone users can still run every drill without Roadmap.
