Major 2025 updates

What changed or received stronger emphasis

The 2025 AHA Guidelines were released October 22, 2025. These are selected high-yield BLS changes, not a substitute for the complete guideline.

Severe choking: adult and child

Use repeated cycles of 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts, starting with back blows, until the object is expelled or the person becomes unresponsive.

Severe choking: infant

Use repeated cycles of 5 back blows followed by 5 chest thrusts. Do not use abdominal thrusts on an infant.

Infant compression technique

Use the heel of one hand or the two-thumb encircling-hands technique. The two-finger technique was removed.

Unified systems and overdose response

A universal Chain of Survival now spans pediatric and adult arrest, and opioid antagonists such as naloxone are incorporated into BLS pathways for suspected opioid emergencies.
