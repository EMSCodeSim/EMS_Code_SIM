# NREMT Skill Sheets and Guided Scenario Entry — Version 1.4.0

## Added
- Searchable NREMT EMT skill-sheet library with official PDF links and local practice tracking.
- Guided scene-size-up layer on every connected patient scenario.
- Questions for PPE, scene safety, patient count, NOI/MOI, resources, cervical-spine precautions, general impression, AVPU, and initial patient priority.
- Non-blocking coaching: missed decisions are saved and reviewed at final debrief rather than preventing scenario progress.
- Scene size-up added to the scenario launcher, progress synchronization, assessment workspace, patient record, and debrief scoring.
- Detailed final debrief for each guided decision, including the learner choice, preferred choice, and clinical rationale.
